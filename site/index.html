              How strategies are read

              From each game's replay file, which records every command a player gives.

              • Opening: units made before Castle Age. 3 or more militia in Dark Age is a Drush; towers built near the enemy is a tower rush; Castle Age within about 5½ minutes of Feudal with fewer than 4 military units is Fast Castle; otherwise the most-made Feudal unit (scouts, archers, men-at-arms, skirms & spears).
              • Castle Age plan: 2 or more extra Town Centers in Castle Age is a boom; otherwise the most-made Castle Age unit type.
              • Attack style: when the army is first ordered into the enemy's base (the starting scout doesn't count). Before 12 minutes is early pressure, 12–22 mid-game, later is a late attacker. Many separate minutes of raids add "constant raids"; 3+ siege units used aggressively add "siege pushes".
              • Defense style: 15+ wall tiles by Feudal Age, 25+ wall tiles in total, 3+ towers or castles near home, a third or more of the army as skirmishers or spearmen.
              • Times are game time; unit counts are units queued, so a few may have been cancelled.

              Watch games in AoE2 on your PC

              One-time setup on each Windows PC that has Age of Empires II: DE. After that, “Watch in AoE2” downloads the game’s replay, opens CaptureAge if you have it, and starts the game.

              ⬇ Download AoE2 Replay for Windows Version —

              1. Unzip AoE2Replay.zip and double-click AoE2Replay.exe. It installs itself for your Windows user (no admin rights).
              2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway. The app isn’t code-signed, so Windows doesn’t recognise it yet.
              3. Click “Watch in AoE2” on any game. The first time, your browser asks to open AoE2 Replay: allow it and tick “Always allow”.
              4. To remove it: Settings → Apps → “AoE2 Replay”, or open it from the Start menu and click Uninstall.

              Game playback

              Three looks: Map (whole map with live soldier dots and a heatmap of where armies or working villagers have been), Detailed (every unit with its type, villager jobs, attack lines and building progress) and Game-style (the same playback drawn like the game). Switch to any player's view to see their fog of war; their card lists the key upgrades they've researched so far. Units' positions are worked out from the orders each player gave, so they're close but not exact.

              Is the data right?

              After every update the job re-checks the dashboard against AoE2 Companion (a separate site that records the same games) and against itself: missing games, ratings, civs and results, duplicates and broken links.

              Check it yourself

              The 12 newest games in the dashboard. Open any one on AoE2 Companion or AoE2 Insights and compare the civ, result, rating change and map.

              What can and can't be checked

              • Games, civs, results, ratings, maps: come straight from the official match records, and are checked against AoE2 Companion after every update.
              • Replay results: when a replay and the match records disagree on who won, the official record is used.
              • Strategies, unit types and positions: worked out from each replay's orders by fixed rules (see "How strategies are read" in Players → Profiles). They can't be checked against another site; to check one, use "Watch in AoE2" in Games and compare.

              Upload a game

              Choose replays of your games. Each one is matched to its game by when it was played, checked, and read at the next update (within 30 minutes).
